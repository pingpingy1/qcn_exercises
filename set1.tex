\section*{Problem 1}
The benefits of applying quantum technology come not from communication speeds,
but rather the usefulness of sharing quantum states itself.
\textbf{Quantum key distribution} (QKD) is one of them:
to ensure computational security, we use the quantum mechanical principle that
perfectly discriminating non-orthogonal states is impossible.
Another is \textbf{quantum teleportation}, where unknown states can be sent far away,
enabling distributed quantum computing systems.
In both of these cases, qubits and ebits (entanglement) are the crucial quantum resources.
